# Crate Crew brief: minimal tracks to recreate

Roundtable group of the Soundsystem, opened 2026-11-08 ET by the coordinator on the owner's instruction: "find some very minimal tracks using just a synth and a drum machine and maybe some basic electro songs too, use them for the DAW, since then we only need to recreate a few instruments to recreate the track."

## Task

Build a catalogue of candidate tracks the DAW can target after Guidance, each with as few distinct instruments as possible and the gear documented where it is known. For every candidate: artist, title, year, label; tempo; the instruments heard (count them: one drum machine, one bass synth, one lead, a vocal sample...) with the evidence; the gear if documented (interviews, liner notes, Equipboard, Vintage Synth Explorer, Discogs credits, documentaries); whether the drums are plainly a TR-808, TR-909, LinnDrum, DMX, CR-78 or unknown; whether the synth is mono or poly, FM or analog, as far as sources say; effects heard (reverb, delay, chorus); the availability of the track (the owner must own or buy it; we commit nothing); and a difficulty score 1 to 5 for recreation with a sampler, an 808, a 909 and two or three synths.

## Method and rules

Cite every claim about gear to a page you saw (web search allowed, at most 10 searches each; mark anything from memory as unverified). Prefer tracks whose instrument count is 3 or fewer. Deliver a table of at least 15 candidates each, ranked by recreation difficulty, then a short list of the 5 you would start with and why. Write only your transcript. No audio is downloaded or committed. Hand back with a 15-line summary.
