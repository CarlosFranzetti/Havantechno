## Sources seen (via search summaries)

1. Reverb, The Synths and Drum Machines of 10 Classic Electro Tracks: https://reverb.com/news/the-synths-and-drum-machines-of-classic-electro (summary: Clear = TR-808 + Korg MS-10 + Pro-One; Egypt, Egypt = Jupiter-8 as synth; Planet Rock = Prophet-5 played live by Robie).
2. Reverb, Juan Atkins' MS-10 and the Dawn of Techno: https://reverb.com/news/juan-atkins-cybotron-the-ms-10-and-the-dawn-of-detroit-techno ; Reverb interview with Atkins: https://reverb.com/news/interview-juan-atkins (summary: "Clear" and "Cosmic Cars" done on TR-808; Atkins listed Pro-One, Roland RS-09, ARP Odyssey, ARP Axxe). The search noted sources disagree on year and spelling of Rik/Rick Davis.
3. Egyptian Lover: Ransom Note https://www.theransomnote.com/?p=11173 (owns at least six 808s); Wikipedia mirrors list TR-808, SH-101, SVC-350 vocoder in one and Jupiter-8 in another (the two conflict). Release 1984, on the album On the Nile.
4. Hashim: https://www.hiphopelectronic.com/no-daw-its-old-school-bangers/electro-songs-hashim-al-naafiysh-the-soul (808 central; bassline synth only guessed: Poly-61, Prophet-5, Juno-60 or Jupiter-8); Qobuz bio (Hashim learned keyboard on a cheap Casio; Cutting Records release 14 Nov 1983).
5. Man Parrish: Ransom Note https://www.theransomnote.com/?p=9141 (first gear 1978: ARP 2600, an Oberheim, a Moog modular; the record was "put together in the editing"); Equipboard https://equipboard.com/pros/man-parrish. No source found naming the drum machine on Hip Hop Be Bop.
6. Mantronix: Wikipedia The Album https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Album_(Mantronix_album) and a 1980s interview quoted in the summary ("Fresh Is the Word" and "Needle to the Groove" were the 808, then he moved to the Emulator SP-12; "we only cut the two tracks using the 808"). No SH-101 found.
7. Newcleus: Songfacts mirror https://dev-www.songfacts.com/facts/newcleus/jam-on-it **[S-weak]** (TR-808 drums, Pro-One bass, TB-303 arpeggios, RS-09); Red Bull Music Academy https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2015/06/key-tracks-newcleus-jam-on-it (Cozmo D's early gear: vocoder, Electro-Harmonix synth, Boss Dr. Rhythm; not confirmed for the 1984 recording).
8. Planet Rock: Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_Rock_(song) , Sound on Sound Classic Tracks https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/classic-tracks-afrika-bambaataa-soulsonic-force-planet-rock , Red Bull https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2013/04/key-tracks-arthur-baker-on-planet-rock (summaries: studio had Fairlight, Memorymoog, TR-808; Robie played Micromoog and Prophet 5 by hand, no sequencer; beat based on Kraftwerk "Numbers"; Fairlight used for an explosion and an orchestra hit).
9. Kraftwerk Computer World: Wikipedia mirror https://en.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Computer_World , MusicRadar https://musicradar.com/news/did-kraftwerk-really-use-a-toy-bee-gees-drum-machine , gearnews https://www.gearnews.com/kraftwerk-synths-sequencers-sounds/ , hiphopelectronic https://www.hiphopelectronic.com/no-daw-its-old-school-bangers/electro-songs-kraftwerk-numbers (summaries: Synthanorma sequencer, Sennheiser VSM-201 and EMS 5000 vocoders, TI language translator voice on Numbers, Friendchip "Mr. Lab" rhythm unit, a toy Bee Gees-branded Mattel rhythm machine; tour gear Prophet-5, Minimoog, Polymoog). No Maestro Rhythm King found.
10. 2 Live Crew / Mr. Mixx: Red Bull https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2016/07/mr-mixx-interview (808 kick; Mixx bought an 808 for $300 at a pawnshop and programmed it like his TR-606; Rudy Ray Moore comedy samples) and https://rockthebells.com/articles/5-pivotal-miami-bass-songs . Release 1986 per most sources, one says Jan 1987.

## The five I would start with, and why

1. **2 Live Crew, Throw the D.** Only one machine, and it is documented as a TR-808 in an interview with its maker (src 10). Plain 808 recreation test with a vocal/sample layer. Difficulty 1.
2. **Run-D.M.C., Sucker M.C.'s.** Only a drum machine and voices. Gear needs a check first (808 or DMX), so treat the drum identification as the test case for the owner's "guess when it is not plainly one" rule. Difficulty 1.
3. **Hashim, Al-Naafiysh.** Plainly 808 plus one or two synths; the synth is undocumented, which exercises the "approximate and say mono or poly" step from the roadmap. Difficulty 2.
4. **Egyptian Lover, Egypt, Egypt.** 808 plus a documented (if conflicting) synth, Jupiter-8 per Reverb; plus a vocoder voice that tests the effects step. Difficulty 2.
5. **Cybotron, Clear.** Best-documented small kit: 808, MS-10, Pro-One (src 1, 2), all mono analog. Gives the cleanest test of the mono-analog synth recreations. Difficulty 2.

Next in line: Kraftwerk Numbers (documented, but the rhythm box is not an 808, so it tests the "unknown drums" path) and Mantronix Needle to the Groove (808 confirmed by Mantronik himself, src 6).
