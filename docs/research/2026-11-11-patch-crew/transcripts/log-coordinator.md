## 2026-11-11 12:59 EST (17:59 UTC) — P1 hand-back (TB-303); the slide ruling

P1-303.md committed. Read: Open303 (MIT, License.txt, Copyright 2009 Robin Schmidt: portable with a THIRD_PARTY.md entry) with constants by file and line; JC303 (GPLv3 around an MIT Open303 core, adds nothing needed); a1k0n/303 has no licence file, so its measurements are a cross-check only. No service manual or schematic was read. Key constants from Open303: a 4-stage coupled ladder with the first coefficient doubled, a 150 Hz highpass in the feedback path, resonance feedback k about 17·r; filter decay tau 200 to 2000 ms, accent decay fixed at 200 ms with a 15 ms smoother; amplitude envelope tau 1230 ms, release 1 ms normal and 50 ms on accent; accent adds up to 4× the filter envelope to the VCA and up to +1 octave of cutoff; slide exponential in Hz with tau = 0.2 × slideTime (12 ms at the 60 ms default), no retrigger, gate held; cutoff knob 314 to 2394 Hz plus an Env Mod table. Open303 has no accent build-up across consecutive accents and no resonance dependence: that circuit behaviour is from P1's memory only, proposed as an optional extension. Open303 and a1k0n disagree on decay (a1k0n 0.1 to 1.1 s to 1/10, Open303 tau 0.2 to 2 s); P1 follows Open303. P1's own Node port predicts a resonance peak of +5.1 dB at 1 kHz and +14 dB at 3 kHz at full resonance (a model result). The panel description is from memory and needs a photo. Open303's "de-click" gain line looks like a bug; P5 is told not to copy it. Hosts P1 could not reach: firstpr.com.au (Robin Whittle's accent notes), timstinchcombe.co.uk (the diode-ladder analysis), kvraudio.com, a1k0n.net and others.

Rulings: (1) frozen names stand; `decay` is a time constant. (2) `ev.slide`, the 303 convention: a step flagged slide holds its gate into the next step and the next note glides from it without retriggering the envelopes; the instrument holds the gate until the next trigger arrives, with a safety release if none comes within two steps. (3) The accent build-up and its resonance dependence are built as an internal extension marked basis 'prior' until a primary source is read; the coordinator proposes allowing the two hosts that hold the primary analyses.
