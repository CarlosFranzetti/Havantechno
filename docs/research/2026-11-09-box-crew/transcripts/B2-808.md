## 1. Circuit facts per voice

### Bass drum
- Trigger pulse (about 1 ms) feeds a bridged-T band-pass network whose ringing is the sound; the same pulse starts an envelope generator that shifts the network's centre frequency for the first few milliseconds (the pitch sweep). [S2] Werner, DAFx-14: https://dafx14.fau.de/papers/dafx14_kurt_james_werner_a_physically_informed,_ci.pdf (snippet only; page not fetched). The Roland-side description: "built from a sine oscillator, low-pass filter, and VCA" is a Wikipedia mirror's wording [S2]; the manual's bridged-T account is the better one.
- Nominal pitch 56 Hz with Decay and Tone at 12 o'clock; tuning shifts slightly with Decay and Tone; longer decay lets the pitch droop at the tail. [S2] search snippet summarising Roland's factory manual (manualslib / support.roland.com, neither fetched). A Japanese Scrapbox note gives "about 56 Hz" independently [S2] https://scrapbox.io/0b5vr/TR-808.
- Accent: "the oscillation frequency is doubled for the duration of half a waveform cycle of the unaccented kick", which makes accented kicks punchier. [S2] same snippet. Tiptop's BD808 manual notes the original has one global accent knob [S2] https://www.polynominal.com/sample-packs/TIPTOP-bd808/Tiptop_Audio_BD808_user-manual.pdf.
- Controls: LEVEL, TONE (lowpass after the resonator, darkens the click and top), DECAY (feedback of the bridged-T). The hardware TR-08 note in a Roland Cloud listing: tone knob for BD, SD and cymbal; decay for BD, cymbal and OH [S2] https://midiamsterdam.nl/roland-cloud-tr-808.html.
- [M] The resonator is a sine near 50 to 60 Hz; the click is the unfiltered trigger edge; with Decay long the voice sustains over a second.

### Snare
- Two bridged-T resonators (one fundamental, one harmonic) plus noise; a trimmer sets the output ratio of tone to noise. [S2] service-manual snippet (manuals.online, not fetched). SNAPPY is the front-panel noise level; TONE the noise high-pass/filter character. A Scrapbox note: two oscillators about 238 Hz and 476 Hz plus white noise [S2] https://scrapbox.io/0b5vr/TR-808. Sound on Sound "Practical Snare Drum Synthesis" covers the 808 snare generator (Figure 8) [S2] https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/practical-snare-drum-synthesis.
- [M] Body decay about 80 to 120 ms, noise (via highpass about 1.5 to 2 kHz) about 150 to 250 ms; a short pitch click on trigger.

### Hand clap
- Output at IC23 pin 2 becomes "narrow pulses during the clap generating cycle" [S2] manual snippet (manualslib page 5/6, not fetched). That agrees with [M]: noise through a band-pass (about 1 kHz, resonant), gated by a short repeated sawtooth-like envelope (three or four bursts roughly 10 ms apart), then a longer exponential tail that is the "reverb". Burst count and timings are unverified. Only LEVEL on the panel.

### Closed and open hats; cymbal; cowbell (the six-oscillator family)
- Cowbell, cymbal, open hat and closed hat all work by filtering and enveloping square-wave outputs of Schmitt-trigger oscillators. [S2] Werner, Abel, Smith, "The TR-808 Cymbal: a Physically-Informed, Circuit-Bendable, Digital Model", ICMC/SMC 2014: https://speech.di.uoa.gr/ICMC-SMC-2014/images/VOL_2/1453.pdf (snippet; also https://zenodo.org/record/850891). Component tolerance sets the oscillator tuning, which is why units differ.
- Open and closed hat share one sound source; triggering CH while OH is sounding turns on Q23 and ends the open hat's decay (the choke). [S2] manual snippet.
- The cowbell uses just two of the six oscillators. [S2] Sound on Sound, https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/synthesizing-cowbells-claves.
- Oscillator frequencies quoted in a snippet: 205.3, 369.6, 304.4, 522.7 Hz plus two ranges (359.4 to 1149.9 and 254.3 to 627.2 Hz) [S2] search result summarising the CCRMA/ICMC paper. [M] the common six-set is 205.3, 304.4, 369.6, 522.7, 540, 800 Hz, and the cowbell pair is 540 and 800 Hz. Treat both as unverified; the bank is not harmonic, which is the point.
- [M] After the bank: band-pass near 3.4 kHz and a high-pass near 7 kHz (hats); cymbal adds a longer envelope and a second filter path; closed hat decay about 40 to 60 ms, open hat 0.3 to 0.6 s with a two-stage decay.
- Controls: CH has LEVEL only; OH has LEVEL and DECAY; CY has LEVEL, TONE, and in the hardware the cymbal tone crossfades between two filter paths (low rumble vs. high sizzle).

### Toms, congas
- "Use the same principle as the bass drum" (multi-feedback bridged-T around IC5); Low Tom mixes pink noise with slightly longer decay. [S2] manual snippet. Conga (LC, MC, HC) are the same type of resonator at higher pitch with a shorter, more clicky envelope. [M] tom fundamentals: LT about 100 Hz, MT about 150 Hz, HT about 200 Hz; congas about 200 to 400 Hz. Panel: TUNING and LEVEL per group (LT/MT/HT share a TUNING, so does LC/MC/HC). [M] panel layout.

### Rim shot, claves
- Both are bridged-T ringers, short. [M] claves: one sharp resonator near 2.5 kHz, decay about 20 to 40 ms, no tuning knob; rim shot: two quick resonators plus a click, about 10 to 30 ms. io-808's author concedes rimshot and cymbal are its weakest [S] https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vincentriemer/io-808/master/README.md. The Sound on Sound cowbells-and-claves article is the reference to read next.

### Maracas
- [M] noise through a high-pass, a very short two-stage envelope (a quick rise, then drop), about 20 to 30 ms. No tuning knob.

### Accent and global behaviour
- One global ACCENT (a step flag; level raises and bass drum gets the frequency-doubling pulse above) [S2]. The engine's `vel()` already gives accent a 1.2 factor (synths.js line 156).
- Output stage: per-voice level knobs and a mix bus; 808 is a polyphonic-by-voice machine (each voice retriggers itself). [M]

## 5. Build specification for A7

Conventions from `app/engine/synths.js` [S, read]: `BUILD.<name> = {spec, build(inst)}`; spec entries `{min, max, value, unit}`; `inst.trigger(t, ev)` with `vel(ev)` (accent x1.2); `voice(ctx, inst)` with `v.osc / v.noise / v.filter / v.gain`; helpers `setV, lin, expo, envDecay(g,t,vel,attack,dec), envHold, series([...])`; output into `inst.pre`; `level` on every voice. Existing kick uses `tune, punch, sweep, decay, tau, hold, attack, click, drive`; clap uses `tone, q, decay, flam, flams, tail, tailDecay`; hats use `decay, tone, ring, metal`. New types are named with an `808_` or `tr808_` prefix [C] so they do not collide: `bd808, sd808, cp808, ch808, oh808, cy808, cb808, lt808, mt808, ht808, lc808, mc808, hc808, rs808, cl808, ma808`. All numbers below are [C] starting values from the [M] facts above, to be fitted by A9.

Shared shape [C]: `level` {0 to 1.5, 1}; `tune` where stated; `decay` as the panel's DECAY; `accent` is read from `ev.accent` (already 1.2x vel). Every voice reads `ev.vel`.

### bd808 (bridged-T kick)
Path: trigger impulse (1 ms) into a resonant bandpass at `tune`, or equivalently a sine oscillator with exponential amplitude, plus a pitch envelope, then a lowpass (TONE), then the saturator.
- `tune` 35 to 90 Hz, 56 (the nominal).
- `decay` 0.1 to 2.0 s, 0.55 (the amplitude time constant; decay to -60 dB).
- `tone` 200 to 5000 Hz, 1500 (lowpass after the body; also scales the click).
- `sweep` 0 to 1, 0.5 (pitch start = tune x (1 + 2 x sweep) falling to tune over about 8 ms).
- `droop` 0 to 1, 0.3 (how much the pitch sinks at the end of long decays; scale with decay).
- `click` 0 to 1, 0.3 (the trigger edge, a 1 ms filtered impulse).
- `drive` 0 to 1, 0.15.
- accent: on `ev.accent` the pitch is doubled for half a cycle (`1/(2 x tune)` s), then falls to `tune`. [S2 source of the behaviour]
- Note: the existing `kick` reaches this with `tune, punch, sweep, decay`; bd808 can reuse `kickEnv`. Parameter-compatible mapping: `punch = tune x 2.5`.

### sd808
Path: two sines (f1 = `tune`, f2 = 2 x f1 approx.; ratio param) each with a fast exp decay (~100 ms), summed through a gain `tone`-controlled, plus white noise through a highpass (~1.8 kHz) with its own envelope (`snappy`), into `inst.pre`.
- `tune` 120 to 400 Hz, 238 [S2 Scrapbox]. `ratio` 1.5 to 3, 2.0 (476/238 [S2]).
- `tone` 0 to 1, 0.5 (body vs. noise brightness; moves the noise highpass 1 to 4 kHz).
- `snappy` 0 to 1, 0.5 (noise level; body fixed).
- `decay` 0.05 to 0.5 s, 0.12 (body); `noiseDecay` 0.08 to 0.6 s, 0.2.
- `click` 0 to 1, 0.3.

### cp808
Path: one noise source through a bandpass (`tone` 1000 Hz, Q about 3), gated by a burst envelope: `bursts` (3 to 4) sawtooth-decay bursts spaced `spacing` (about 10 ms), then an exponential tail `tail`.
- `tone` 500 to 3000 Hz, 1000; `q` 0.5 to 8, 3; `bursts` 1 to 6, 4; `spacing` 0.004 to 0.03 s, 0.01; `burstDecay` 0.003 to 0.02 s, 0.008; `decay` 0.05 to 0.6 s, 0.2 (the tail); `tail` 0 to 1, 0.5.
- Reuse: this is the existing `clap` with `flam`=spacing, `flams`=bursts-1, `tailDecay`=decay. A7 may implement cp808 as a preset of `clap` plus a narrower Q rather than a new builder.

### Metal bank (ch808, oh808, cy808, cb808)
Shared helper `bank808(v, t, end, freqs)`: N square oscillators (50 percent duty) at the given frequencies, summed. Frequencies [M, S2 partial] 205.3, 304.4, 369.6, 522.7, 540, 800 Hz, scaled by `tune` ratio (1.0 default); expose `spread` 0 to 1 (0=exact, 1 = jitter each by up to +/-5 percent, per-unit tolerance behaviour, S2).
- ch808: bank (all six) into bandpass 3400 Hz Q about 1, then highpass 7000 Hz; envelope `decay` 0.01 to 0.15 s, 0.045. `tone` 0 to 1 (moves the highpass 5 to 9 kHz). Chokes any live oh808 (hook into the same choke map as the sampler's `chokeGroups`; one `hat808` group). [S2 behaviour]
- oh808: same path; `decay` 0.1 to 1.5 s, 0.4 (the panel DECAY knob), two-stage envelope (fast to -12 dB in 30 ms, then exponential); stops at the next ch808.
- cy808: bank into two parallel filter paths (bandpass ~3.4 kHz, highpass ~7 kHz), mixed by `tone` (0 = low path, 1 = high path); `decay` 0.4 to 3 s, 1.5; with a 5 ms attack.
- cb808: only the 540 and 800 Hz oscillators into a bandpass at about 2640 Hz (Q 1) then a slight lowpass; envelope two-stage (fast ~15 ms to 30 percent, then ~0.3 s decay); `tune` ratio 0.7 to 1.4, 1; `decay` 0.1 to 1 s, 0.35; `level`.

### lt808, mt808, ht808, lc808, mc808, hc808
Same resonator as bd808, higher pitch, short pitch sweep; low tom mixes a little pink noise. Params: `tune` (LT 100 Hz, MT 150, HT 200, LC 200, MC 280, HC 380 [M]), `decay` (toms 0.2 to 0.6 s, default 0.3; congas 0.1 to 0.3, 0.14), `sweep` 0 to 1 (0.3), `click` 0 to 1 (0.2), `noise` 0 to 1 (0.15 LT, 0 others). Panel has one TUNING per group of three: offer `tuneGroup` as an offset in the Box UI, and `tune` per voice in the engine. [M panel layout]

### cl808 (claves), rs808 (rim shot), ma808 (maracas)
- cl808: sine at 2500 Hz (`tune` 1500 to 4000), decay `decay` 0.01 to 0.1 s (0.03), exponential, no sweep; slight second partial at 1.5x quiet.
- rs808: two resonators (1700 Hz and 480 Hz, `tune` offsets both) plus a 2 ms click; `decay` 0.01 to 0.08 s (0.025). Known weak spot in io-808 [S]; leave room for A9 to retune.
- ma808: noise through highpass 5000 Hz, envelope attack 3 ms then `decay` 0.01 to 0.1 s (0.03).

### Group/kit layer
- A `kit808` track type holds the sixteen voices and routes the 808's mix: an `accent` bus flag, `level` per voice, mono sum, optional per-voice `pan`. Choke map: oh808 stops on ch808 only. [S2]
- Also expose a per-voice `sat` (drive) to match Roland Cloud's gain-overdrive extension [S2], default 0.

## 7. Verification plan for A9

Inputs: the owner's reference one-shots (808 BD, SD, CP, CH, OH, CY, CB, LT/MT/HT, LC/MC/HC, RS, CL, MA), preferably one per voice at default knobs plus BD and OH at two decay settings and SD at SNAPPY low/high.
Method, using `tools/render.js` for the offline render of the voice (default params, vel 1) and `tools/score.py` measures:
1. Alignment: trim leading silence at -60 dBFS, normalise both to peak 1.
2. Per-voice pass criteria (first guesses, to be tuned):
   - **BD**: fundamental (FFT peak in the first 150 ms after the click) within 3 Hz of the reference; RT60 (time to -60 dB) within 20 percent; peak pitch at 10 ms within 15 Hz; spectral centroid 0 to 100 ms within 15 percent.
   - **SD**: two partials (peaks below 600 Hz) within 5 percent of the reference; noise-to-body energy ratio within 3 dB; decay time of the noise within 25 percent.
   - **CP**: burst count and spacing from the envelope (peak-picking of 2 ms RMS) equal and within 3 ms; tail RT within 25 percent; centroid within 20 percent.
   - **CH/OH/CY/CB**: log-spectrum distance (mean of |dB difference| over 1 to 16 kHz, 1/3-octave bands) under 6 dB; band-energy envelope over time within 3 dB at 20 ms steps; for cowbell the two peaks at 540 and 800 Hz and the band-pass peak within 5 percent.
   - **Toms/congas**: fundamental within 3 percent; RT within 20 percent.
   - **RS/CL/MA**: spectral centroid within 15 percent; length (to -40 dB) within 25 percent.
3. Choke test: render a CH over an OH; the OH must be below -60 dB within 10 ms of the CH.
4. Accent test: bd808 with `accent`; initial pitch about 2x the unaccented (S2 behaviour), level +1.6 dB (vel x1.2).
5. Output a table (voice, metric, ref, ours, pass) and a "tuning delta" list that tells A7 which parameter to move (e.g. the fundamental error tunes `tune`; RT error tunes `decay`).
6. Do not auto-tune to the reference; ship the model, record the delta, and let the owner accept. If the owner's references are of 808 emulations rather than hardware, say so in the report; match anyway.
7. Before this plan can score hats, cymbal and cowbell properly, the oscillator frequencies need confirming from the Werner paper (hosts in section 0) or from analysing the owner's cymbal sample (peak-pick the first 50 ms).

### Confirmed [S] (upgrade of first-pass tags)
- Six oscillators (Schmitt-trigger, HD14584 hex inverter, 5 V): "nominal frequencies (or ranges) of 205.3, 369.6, 304.4, 522.7, 359.4 to 1149.9, and 254.3 to 627.2 Hz. Oscillators #5 to 6 have internal trimpots ... for factory tuning to specific frequencies (800 and 540 Hz, respectively)"; duty 47.98 percent, amplitude 5 V. [S] cymbal paper section 3 (footnote: before serial #000300 oscillators 5 and 6 used different parts). So the list 205.3, 304.4, 369.6, 522.7, 540, 800 Hz is now confirmed, in oscillator order 205.3, 369.6, 304.4, 522.7, 800, 540.
- Band-pass filters on the summed bank: "centre frequency around 3440 Hz" (BPF #1) and "around 7100 Hz" (BPF #2). [S] section 4. Three high-pass Sallen-Key filters follow the three swing-type VCAs; HPF #3 corner "around 10500 Hz" with resonance. [S] section 9.
- Cowbell, cymbal, open hat, closed hat all "work by filtering and enveloping square-wave outputs of Schmitt-trigger oscillators" [S]; cowbell "uses just two of the six pulse-wave oscillators", through a band-pass, VCAs sharing one contour generator [S] SOS cowbell article. Cymbal: four envelope signals to three swing-type VCAs, three bands, each with its own decay; the decay knob changes the RC of the envelope for the band it reaches; the tone control mainly changes the attenuation of the third band (non-orthogonal, like a tone stack) [S] cymbal paper sections 7, 10. Open and closed hat: same source (first pass, [S2], not re-checked).
- Kick: 1 ms trigger pulse into the pulse shaper and the bridged-T band-pass; the same pulse starts an envelope generator that, for about 5 to 6 ms, switches the bridged-T from a lower to a higher centre frequency and Q "by more than an octave", "punchier and crisper"; then a retriggering pulse (C39, R161, D52) restores amplitude. A separate "pitch sigh" comes from leakage through R161. Model centre frequency "approximately 49.5 Hz", "close to ... Roland's typical and variable tuning chart (56 Hz)". Decay is VR6 (frequency effect and decay), tone VR5. [S] DAFx-14 sections 2, 5, 8. Snare, toms/congas and rim shot/clave all use bridged-T networks like the kick; handclap, cowbell, cymbal and hats use bridged-T Zobel networks as band-pass filters [S] footnotes 13, 15.
- Snare: trigger drives two bridged-T oscillators; a contour (the "snappy" signal) is attenuated and added to the trigger before the two oscillators; a contour-gated white-noise VCA, high-passed, is mixed with the oscillators; the oscillator mix ratio is fixed by factory component values [S] SOS snare article ("The TR808 Snare").

### Corrected (first pass was wrong or misattributed)
1. **Kick accent.** The first pass said "accent doubles the frequency for half a cycle" [S2]. Neither paper says that. Werner says the CPU combines timing and accent into a common trigger whose ON voltage is the global accent level, which sets the pulse amplitude and affects downstream nonlinear parts; the circuit's real attack behaviour (more than an octave of centre-frequency shift for about 6 ms) happens on every hit, accented or not [S]. The doubling claim stays unverified (it came from a search snippet of the Roland manual, which I could not read). Drop it from the spec; use accent as amplitude/drive, plus a bit more of the attack shift.
2. **Cowbell 587 and 845 Hz** (I did not use them, but the first-pass search note cited a "587/845" pair): those are Gordon Reid's measurement of the Roland CR8000, not the 808 [S] SOS. The 808 pair is 540 and 800 Hz, band-pass 2.64 kHz is Reid's CR8000 figure ("a centre frequency of 2.64 kHz worked well") [S], not an 808 measurement.
3. **Snare 180 and 330 Hz** in my search query came from Reid's own patch ("single VCA and contour generator for the 180 Hz and 330 Hz partials") [S], not the 808. 238 and 476 Hz are constants in io-808's code (below), not a circuit measurement; their origin in io-808 is not stated. Treat as unverified for the real 808.
4. **Hat oscillator set in io-808 differs from the circuit.** io-808 hats use `[263, 400, 421, 474, 587, 845]` Hz (squareOscBank.js line 5), which is not Werner's six. Its cowbell uses 540 and 800 (correct). Do not port the hat list.
5. **BPF "about 3.4 kHz / HPF about 7 kHz" for hats [M]**: the circuit has band-pass at 3440 and 7100 Hz and high-pass corners near 10.5 kHz (the first pass swapped the roles). Whether the hats use all three bands the way the cymbal does is not established by what I read (the paper is about the cymbal).
6. **Tag changes:** kick centre 56 Hz nominal [S2, Roland chart as cited by Werner: "typical and variable ... (56 Hz)"] is now [S] as Werner's citation; the 49.5 Hz is Werner's model value [S]. All "[M] oscillator frequencies" in section 1 are [S] as above. The cymbal's three-band structure is [S]. Snare noise, claves, rim, toms, congas, maracas and clap timings remain [M]/io-808 constants.

### io-808 constants (MIT; file:line at the commit above)
bassDrum.js: `FREQ_AMT = 50` (9), `START_FREQ = 48` (10), `vcfFreq = 200 + tone*20` (15), `decayTime = decay*5 + 50` ms (16), sine VCO at 48 Hz, exponential frequency envelope attack 0.11 ms, start 48 plus 50 Hz (37-43), linear amplitude envelope attack 2 ms to decayTime (44), a 1 ms pulse low-passed at 5 kHz gain 0.8 added as the click (basics/pulseTrigger.js 9, 19, 23), lowpass Q 1 (24), soft clipper 0.6 (34). Default UI values: level 75, tone 50, decay 50 (initialState.js 56-58).
snareDrum.js: `highOscFreq = 476` (7), `lowOscFreq = 238` (8), sines; noise high-pass at `tone*100 + 800` (13), `snappyEnvAmt = snappy/200` (14), noise envelope linear attack 0.1 ms, decay 75 ms, amount 0.5 (35), snappy envelope linear 0.1 ms, 50 ms, amount snappy/200 applied to the oscillator VCA (36). Defaults level 75, tone 50, snappy 50.
maracasHandclap.js: maracas high-pass 5000 Hz (24), envelope linear 0.2 ms attack, 30 ms decay, amount 0.5 (27). Clap: band-pass 1000 Hz (43), saw envelope of four bursts every 10 ms (basics/sawEnvGenerator.js lines 1, 12-16; peak levels `1 - i/2` = 1, 0.5, 0, -0.5, so bursts three and four are zero and negative: likely a bug), plus a "reverb" envelope linear 0.2 ms attack, 115 ms decay, amount 0.75 (49).
cowbell.js: two square VCOs 800 and 540 Hz (10-11), band-pass 2640 Hz, Q 1 (7-8), short envelope 15 ms (13), long envelope 400 ms, amount 0.25 of total (14-15, 39-51).
hiHat.js / clsdHat.js / openHat.js: bank of six squares at amplitude 0.3 each (squareOscBank.js 6), band-pass 10000 Hz then VCA then high-pass 8000 Hz (hiHat.js 6-7, 28-31), closed decay 50 ms (clsdHat.js 7), open decay `decay*3.6 + 90` ms (openHat.js 7), linear envelope.
cymbal.js: three bands: filters 5000, 10000, 8000 Hz (7-9), decays 700 + 8.5 x decay ms (low), 400 ms (mid), 150 ms (high) (10-11, 16), tone mixes the low and high band amounts as 0.666 x ratio (18-21). 
claveRimshot.js: `RIM_CLAVE_FREQ = 1750`, `CLAVE_FREQ = 2450`, `RIM_FREQ = 480` (9-11); clave triangle 2450 Hz through a band-pass at 2450, exponential envelope 40 ms amount 0.7 (47); rim: 480 Hz sine through band-pass 480, a swing VCA with a 10 ms linear envelope amount 1.7 (31), then a high-pass at 480 Hz; rim also uses a 1750 Hz triangle through a high-pass at 2450.
tomConga.js: `[osc Hz at tuning 100, osc Hz at tuning 0]` and [osc decay, noise decay] ms: low conga [220,165],[180,200]; low tom [100,80],[200,200]; mid conga [310,250],[100,155]; mid tom [160,120],[130,155]; high conga [455,370],[180,125]; high tom [220,165],[200,125] (tomConga.js 10-41); frequency = tuning/100 x (high - low) + low (51); pink noise low-passed at 10 kHz at gain 0.2 (64, 74); 1 ms click at gain 0.3 (59).
These are one developer's tuned-by-ear values (the README says the sounds are close but not exact and that cymbal and rimshot are the weakest [S]). Use them as a prior, not a reference.

### Spec updates for A7 (replaces the defaults in section 5 where they differ)
- **bd808**: `tune` default 49.5 Hz (Werner's model; Roland chart 56 Hz is the range centre; range 35 to 90). Pitch envelope: replace my `sweep`/`droop` with a measured-style attack shift: centre frequency ratio about 2.0 (more than an octave) returning to `tune` over 6 ms [S]; an extra slow "sigh" of a few percent over the decay, `droop` default 0.1. Amplitude: 1 ms trigger pulse; after the 6 ms the amplitude needs a retrigger bump (the paper's retriggering pulse) so the 6 ms shift does not leave a dip: model with the existing `kickEnv` and a small second impulse at t + 6 ms. `decay` default 0.35 s (io-808: 50 + 5 x 50 = 300 ms at the mid setting, range 50 to 550 ms; the real knob reaches over a second at maximum, [M]). `tone` as a lowpass 200 to 2200 Hz (io-808: 200 + 20 x tone), default 1200 Hz. Remove the "frequency doubling on accent" rule; `accent` raises trigger amplitude (use the engine's 1.2).
- **ch808, oh808, cy808** (shared bank): oscillators 205.3, 369.6, 304.4, 522.7, 800, 540 Hz, 47.98 percent duty, `spread` per-oscillator tolerance jitter defaults 0. Filters: bandpass 3440 Hz and bandpass 7100 Hz on the bank, then high-pass about 10500 Hz (Q about 1.5) as a first guess; cy808 uses three bands with separate decays (io-808 priors: low 700+ ms, mid 400 ms, high 150 ms; the real values are the RC times in the cymbal paper, which I did not extract) and `tone` scaling the third band. Hat decay defaults: closed 0.05 s, open 0.09 to 0.45 s (io-808 priors, [S] constants but not circuit-measured).
- **cb808**: oscillators 540 and 800 Hz square [S], band-pass centre 2640 Hz is a prior from Reid's CR8000 and io-808 [S]; envelope two-stage (15 ms fast, 400 ms exponential with weight 0.25) [io-808 prior], single contour generator [S].
- **sd808**: keep 238 and 476 Hz as defaults [io-808 constants], marked prior; add `tone` as the noise high-pass 800 to 900+ Hz and `snappy` as the noise amount and the snappy contour added to the trigger (matches the SOS description: snappy contour added to the trigger so it also kicks the oscillators) [S]. Noise decay 75 ms, snappy contour 50 ms [io-808 priors].
- **cp808**: band-pass 1000 Hz, four bursts spaced 10 ms, reverb tail about 115 ms [io-808 priors]. Fix the burst levels to a non-negative saw pattern (1, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5 or fit to a sample).
- **toms/congas**: use the io-808 table as default `tune` end points and decays (above), with `tune` knob mapping between them; start of pitch is a prior.
- **cl808/rs808/ma808**: clave 2450 Hz [io-808 prior, matches my 2.5 kHz memory], rim 480 Hz plus 1750 Hz, maracas high-pass 5000 Hz, 30 ms: all priors.

### Verification plan changes (A9)
- The oscillator frequencies are now documented, so the metal-voice check becomes a peak-finding test: analyse the first 20 ms of the reference closed hat or cymbal; expect energy clusters from the square-wave harmonics of 205.3, 304.4, 369.6, 522.7, 540, 800 Hz as shaped by band-passes at 3440 and 7100 Hz; the cowbell reference should show fundamentals at 540 and 800 Hz (a roughly 1:1.48 ratio). Fail if the dominant partials are more than 3 percent off.
- Add a kick attack test: pitch track the first 10 ms for a rise above the settled pitch by roughly 2x, settling within about 6 ms, then settled pitch within 5 Hz of 49.5 to 56 Hz at default.
- Remove the accent-pitch-doubling test; replace with accent = louder and slightly punchier (level up, no pitch change after 10 ms).
