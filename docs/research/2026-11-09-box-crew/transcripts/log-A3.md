## Batch 2: drum voice faces (A12's 27 voices) and fm
- **27 definitions, 5 heroes** (all in facedefs.js, one `drumFace(type, skin, hero, glyph, groups, strip)` table): `heroDKick` (bd808, bd909: pitch glide on a log Hz axis over the amplitude envelope, click spike; 909 uses start/end/time-constant/hold), `heroDTom` (the six 808 toms and congas, the three 909 toms: pitch sweep, amp decay, noise tail; base Hz per voice is a display hint: lt 100, mt 150, ht 200, lc 200, mc 280, hc 380), `heroDSnare` (sd808, sd909: two resonator lines at f and f x ratio plus the noise band), `heroDMetal` (ch/oh/cy/cb 808, ch/oh/cr/rd 909: the six bank lines at 205.3..800 Hz x tune, spread ticks, the bandpass/highpass curve over them, then the envelope: closed, two-stage open, attack cymbal, two-stage bell, hold-and-decay 909 with crush marks), `heroDBurst` (cp808, cp909: burst train and tail), `heroDShort` (rs808, rs909, cl808, ma808: resonator lines and a short envelope). Controls come in signal-order sections; drive and level in OUT.
- **Looks:** `layout.skin` (new, faces.js): 808 orange-red (#ff5a36 on #2b0e08), 909 grey-black (#cfd3da on #16171a). It sets --fam-ink/dim/wash on the face and overrides the hero palette, so it does not depend on the host's family table (which does not know these types; Faces maps them to drums).
- **Priors:** any spec entry with `basis: 'prior'` gets a small dotted ring (top right of the control, aria-hidden, no text, the spec `label` as hover title). Generic, so it also shows in the fallback and the "more" drawer. Fitted (paper, source) and choice controls have none.
