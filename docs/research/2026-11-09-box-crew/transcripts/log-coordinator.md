## 2026-11-09 04:36 EST (09:36 UTC) — B3 third pass (TR-909 Service Notes, 1984)

B3-909.md (365 lines) committed. The document is Roland's TR-909 Service Notes, first edition, 15 June 1984, 16 scanned pages at low resolution; illegible values are marked unresolved, not guessed.
