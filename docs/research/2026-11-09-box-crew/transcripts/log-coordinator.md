## 2026-11-08 23:50 EST (04:50 UTC) — L1 hand-back (classification methods); rulings

L1-methods.md committed. Separation runs in the sandbox: UVR's KUIELab MDX-Net ONNX models (drums, bass, other) download from a GitHub release and run at about 2× real time on this CPU; against exact stems (the V3 mainA3 solos summed) SDR is drums 9.7, bass 7.5, other 15.9 dB; against the engine's real mix the bass scores 0 dB because the master is not linear (the solos sum to the mix at only 8.9 dB). Methods tested on engine-labelled sounds: synth or sample by nearest-twin cross-correlation of aligned hits (noise voices 0.10 to 0.28 read synth, all five sampler tracks 1.00 read sample, a sampled hat stayed identifiable in the mix at 0.99 and in the separated stem at 0.95; limit: deterministic synthesis also reads 1.00, so "sample" is capped at low confidence unless the formant test or a library match supports it); mono or poly by iterative pitch estimation and cancellation (stab and pad 6 of 6 notes, bass and sub 1); FM or subtractive by fitting both models per note (10 of 11, ratio recovered every time; single notes only); reverb by Schroeder backward integration after the last hit (presence 9 of 9, RT60 within 0.1 s of the engine's own, pre-delay 15.9 against 15 ms; fails on a panned hat and on the full mix mid-song); shared against element-specific reverb separable, built-in against outboard not readable from audio; kick glide recovered (53.3 Hz end, 267 start, 22.3 ms against 53, 265, 22); instrument matching by rules 7 of 10, with the proposed method being to fit the engine's own instruments to the target and use the remaining distance as confidence.
